Profile

the person behind the work

A multidisciplinary designer from Ahmedabad with a background in Communication Design, industry experience in UI/UX, and a current focus on Strategic Design Management at NID Gandhinagar. My practice brings together visual communication, digital experiences, design thinking, and the study of human behaviour to understand challenges from both human and organisational perspectives. I’m interested in how design processes, behavioural insights, and strategic thinking can inform decisions and shape meaningful outcomes across products, services, and organisations.
