Launch Strategy Five moves
to lift-off.
01 Define & Nail the USP

Solidify the brand's core message as the "High-Protein, Delicious, On-The-Go Fuel."

02 Micro-Influencer & Ambassador Seeding

Send free product boxes to 50-100 micro-influencers (under 25k followers) in the fitness, nutrition, and busy-mom/professional niches.

03 E-Commerce & Subscription Launch

Launch the brand's website with a strong introductory offer (e.g., "Launch Bundle: Try All 4 Flavors + Free Shipping") and prominently feature a subscription option ("Never Run Out of Fuel – Save 15%").

Launch Bundle Try All 4 Flavors
+ Free Shipping
04 Hyper-Local Sampling & Pop-Ups

Execute high-impact, one-day sampling pop-ups at two key locations: a major local gym/fitness studio and a high-traffic business district/college campus.

05 Digital Retargeting & Feedback Loop

Immediately launch retargeting ads to anyone who visited the website or watched the influencer videos. Simultaneously, send a post-purchase survey to all customers.
