Wear yournarrative.

NOU is a men’s footwear label handcrafted in 100% Indian leather. I took it from market research to a brand, an identity system and an e‑commerce store.

The brief

In 2024 India spent ₹2,18,904 crore on footwear. Buyers wanted shoes that last, but the shelf made them choose: premium or handcrafted. NOU was made to be both.

Six chapters, each named for a stage of making a shoe: 01the last, 02the pattern, 03clicking, 04closing, 05lasting and 06finishing.
